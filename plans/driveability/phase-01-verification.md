# Phase 01 — Physical world verification

Implementation date: 2026-09-07. Scope: static world, clearance, collision inspection and lifecycle. No Phase 02 vehicle, Phase 03 driving interaction or Phase 04 integrated qualification is included.

## Architecture and decisions

- Runtime: pinned official `@dimforge/rapier3d-compat` 0.20.0, dynamically imported by a scene-owned session. The compatibility package embeds WASM, avoiding a second asset path/init contract. The public [initialization guide](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/getting_started_js/) and installed 0.20.0 declarations were checked; some public guide pages are still labeled JavaScript 0.17. The package's actual declarations govern signatures.
- Each world has **six fixed colliders and zero rigid bodies**: ground cuboid (top y=0), the complete indexed building triangle mesh, and four artificial boundary cuboids. No road collider, kerb, scale change, hull or source-QA-derived geometry is added. Base caps remain part of the closed building mesh and coincide with ground under occupied footprints. There is only one support collider on clear road, off-road and courtyard areas.
- [Triangle meshes](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/colliders/#triangle-meshes-and-polylines) retain concavity and holes but do not establish solid point containment. Roof triangles are indexed as the union of ground-to-roof triangular prisms. Box overlap tests vertical intervals and all triangle/rectangle separating axes. This detects enclosed boxes, enclosed triangles and edge crossings; testing only the center/corners or Rapier surface intersection is insufficient. Query X/Z/height use the same Float32 coordinates as rendering/collision, with Float64 arithmetic and 10 μm touch tolerance.
- Before publishing physics, the runtime checks flat cap/wall classes, matched projected floor/roof caps, upward roofs/downward bases and welded directed edge closure. Unsupported/open/sloped meshes fail physical readiness without changing artifact validity. This is an index/compatibility check of artifact faces; the browser imports no compiler, triangulator, OSM, report or QA geometry code. No schema-v1 extension is required.
- The play envelope is the X/Z AABB of actual clipped-road vertices, inset **1 m per side**. Collapsed envelopes fail physics initialization. Mesh-union bounds, building/graph outliers and the renderer's padding have no effect. Walls are outside the envelope, 1 m thick, spanning y=-1 to y=20. Amber ground edges/uprights remain visible when source or generated geometry is hidden. The rectangle is an artificial simulation limit: it can exclude streets, include unmodeled land/water, and does not certify the projected acquisition polygon at its corners.
- `clearance(worldAabb, boundaryMargin)` returns ground/building/boundary reasons. The caller must supply a conservative **complete** world AABB at its current orientation, including any wheels/suspension/motion margin; the API does not claim an oriented vehicle fit or certify pavement support. Invalid/nonfinite/inverted inputs throw. A box below y=0 is rejected; the supported vertical ceiling is y=20. `occupied` also supports zero-width point boxes and checks roof/headroom overlap. `needsRecovery` identifies any horizontally escaped full box, nonfinite box, or vertical envelope outside [-2,20]. Phase 02 must stop and reset/revalidate an escaped body, choose braking/start margins and establish actual road support. No clamping, teleport, spawn algorithm, controller or reset vehicle exists here.

## Contacts and lifecycle

World timestep is 1/60 s; catch-up is capped at five steps per frame and excess time discarded. Static geometry renders directly in artifact coordinates, without interpolation or body-pose copying; Phase 02 must add the pose synchronization policy. Worlds begin paused. Explicit resume resets the timestamp and accumulator; blur/visibility changes pause, hidden pages cancel the renderer RAF, and returning remains paused. Rendering and physics use one existing RAF owner.

Probe evidence uses 0.5 m cuboids, gravity -9.81 m/s², locked rotations, CCD, initial velocities up to 10 m/s **per moving axis** (14.14 m/s diagonal magnitude), and a 2 cm penetration tolerance. Ground/building/boundary friction is 0.8, probe friction 0, default average combination, and restitution 0. Internal-edge correction (`TriMeshFlags.FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES`, which merges duplicate vertices without deleting triangles) avoids false lift at wall triangulation seams. The world allows up to eight [CCD substeps](https://rapier.rs/javascript3d/classes/IntegrationParameters.html#maxCcdSubsteps). A corner regression also varies approach speed (3/5/10 m/s per axis) and four initial distances, preventing a single favorable timestep alignment from being the evidence.

During investigation, stripping building base faces produced worse corner penetration, despite the ground occupying the same height. Retaining the closed mesh plus the declared contact settings passes the probes. This evidence supports preserving the exact included mesh; it does not establish rotating chassis contacts, wheel behavior or a production speed limit. The future vehicle must enable chassis CCD and rerun its own dimensions, rotation, suspension and speed checks.

Initialization has a 30 s logical deadline. Disposal/deadline prevents later module or WASM completion from constructing a world; partial construction and step failures free it. Failure is distinct from READY and leaves orbit/camera/QA rendering available. [`World.free()`](https://rapier.rs/javascript3d/classes/World.html#free) releases its owned WASM resources. Unmount/navigation dispose physics, render resources, controls, ResizeObserver, RAF and listeners. A browser bfcache restoration reloads the disposed page through one once-only pageshow listener. The shared module/WASM code remains page-owned, and linear memory can retain its high-water capacity after world disposal.

Coverage includes ground support (road/off-road/courtyard), enclosed building occupancy versus surface non-overlap, concavity, whole-box crossings, roof clearance, inner/outer walls, glancing/corner contacts, all four boundaries, full-envelope recovery, malformed meshes, all five prepared worlds, partial construction failure, repeated free, late async success/failure/unmount, timeout, readable download-failure feedback, pause/clock behavior at 30/60/120/144 Hz and mock-renderer/listener/observer/RAF cleanup. Eight scene cycles and ten physical-world cycles assert exactly-once cleanup; these are ownership tests, not a claim to measure all browser/GPU memory.

It loads prepared bytes only, adds one bounded test probe, steps 600 times and frees each world, then runs 20 dense-cell create/step/dispose cycles. No prepared product changed.
