# Phase 04 — Qualification

## Goal

Establish exactly which physical driving behaviors the stage supports, with reproducible evidence and explicit limits.

## Deliverable

A qualification record linking tested artifact identities/hashes, runtime/vehicle configuration, repeatable physical scenarios, desktop/touch observations, measured performance, regressions, and unresolved limitations. This record is produced during implementation, not by the planning task.

## Success criteria

- Assess all five [prepared corpus cells](../parameterized-zone-generation/qualification.md) one at a time with the same runtime rules. Proposed roles: Cambridge for initial driving and courtyards; Chicago for a contrasting grid; Lucca for dense concavity/courtyards; Trafalgar for regression and larger mesh cost; Tower Bridge for explicit flattened-crossing and exclusion limits. Choose actual maneuvers only after checking physical clearance, without interpreting a place name as terrain or driving proof.
- At least two contrasting cells complete normal driving in identified test areas. Across the matrix, record spawn/reset, straight travel and turns, stopping/reverse, stationary and moving wall contact, open-courtyard/occupied-building queries, off-road return, cell edges, no-safe-spawn, stuck/overturned recovery, and escaped/fallen recovery. Controlled fixtures may supply rare failure cases; no obligation to drive every street or cross an enclosed courtyard wall is implied.
- Verify the same maneuvers across agreed render cadences and forward/reverse speed limits, with stated tolerances. Review observed motion/collision rather than relying on a READY label, graph counts, or screenshots alone. Source limitations identify the affected scenario and evidence; physics defects must be fixed or explicitly renegotiated before stage exit.
- Exercise keyboard and touch sessions, camera occlusion, field focus, pointer cancellation, pause/hidden-tab resume, repeated reset, zone navigation, async initialization failure/unmount, and service-unavailable behavior. Prepared-zone driving requires no generation API; existing generation success/failure/reconnection behavior remains intact.
- Measure collider setup/start latency, sustained frame and physics-step times, and memory/resource behavior during an agreed-duration drive and repeated reset/load/dispose cycles on named desktop and mobile targets. Compare with budgets agreed before these acceptance runs, using earlier phase measurements. Record cold initialization and dense cells; phone performance requires representative hardware evidence, not viewport emulation alone.
- Run appropriate simulation/input/lifecycle tests and the existing offline corpus, test, static, production-build, and browser regressions at implementation time. Confirm no compiler/source processing enters the browser and no unrelated generated bytes change. Live acquisition is optional evidence, not a requirement for repeatability.

## Exit condition

The [stage success criteria and exit condition](./README.md#stage-success-criteria) are demonstrated by the recorded results. Every corpus case has an explicit outcome, all required physical and interaction checks pass within their declared envelope, performance meets the agreed supported-device budgets, and remaining geographic limitations are stated without claiming legal routes or universal cell support.

## Out of scope

Legal-route or all-street certification, global map quality, new source acquisition as a release gate, deterministic cross-platform physics replay, adjacent-cell driving, broad hardware guarantees, and expanding the stage to repair geographic fidelity.

## Dependency and planning agreement

Depends on the verified [Driving interaction](./phase-03-driving-interaction.md) exit and evidence accumulated in earlier phases. Discuss and detail the qualification implementation plan with the user when ready under the [stage working agreement](./README.md#working-agreement). Do not substitute historical compiler/rendering checks for new physical evidence or weaken acceptance silently after seeing results.
