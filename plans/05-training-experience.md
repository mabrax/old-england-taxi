# 05 — Training Experience

## Goal

Build the route-learning experience on top of the functioning simulator.

## Scope

- Select an origin and destination inside a loaded zone.
- Present a short study view before driving.
- Reveal orientation, distance, landmarks, and reference route progressively.
- Continue after mistakes and detect departures and recoveries.
- Finish with a concise session review and route repetition.

## Exit condition

A complete session helps the user recall the route, identify difficult decisions, recover from errors, and repeat the practice with fewer aids.
