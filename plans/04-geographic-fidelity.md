# 04 — Geographic Fidelity

## Goal

Add the geographic details that materially improve recognition and orientation.

## Scope

- Elevation from a DEM and terrain-following roads.
- Correct separation of bridges, tunnels, and road layers.
- Building parts and common roof shapes where OSM provides them.
- Parks, water, barriers, street names, and selected recognizable landmarks.

## Exit condition

The chosen zones preserve important slopes, crossings, silhouettes, and landmarks well enough to support spatial memory.

## Deferred

Photorealism and exhaustive architectural reconstruction.
